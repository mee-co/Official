What is mee?

mee is a unified digital ecosystem built around a single, personal @mee.com address — your key to every mee service. Under one roof, it brings together music, communication, and content: mee Records nurtures independent artists, while meeMusic delivers audio and video streaming. Seipe connects you through high-quality video calls, Switch reimagines social networking, UpDown offers smart messaging, and PlugIn provides a browser designed for the meeian lifestyle. Every product is linked to your mee identity, making the experience seamless and personal. Founded by Abdullah Al Mustafa, mee stands for creative freedom, digital ownership, and an ecosystem that revolves around you.
